Welcome to Silicon Valley AI Nexus!

Silicon Valley AI Nexus is a next-generation Silicon Valley community exploring AI's profound impact on technology, economy, society, ethics, and humanity—and translating that exploration into real-world action.

We have evolved into the premier Silicon Valley AI community bridging Korean and global tech ecosystems. Our community has grown and united by a shared conviction – AI must advance human flourishing, not diminish it.

📌 2026 — the year AI Nexus became an institution.
Now a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, seated at the table for the San Francisco AI Summit, broadcasting our forums worldwide, and running SIX member-led interest groups between monthly Chapters.

Everything we do is guided by three core pillars!

  • Explore & Lead — World-class forums, the AI & Humanity Council, and thought leadership shaping AI's most consequential questions
  • Elevate Community — Mentorship, startup showcases, professional connections, and leadership opportunities that amplify every voice
  • Give Back to Society — Policy engagement, academic partnerships, and public discourse ensuring AI's benefits reach beyond Silicon Valley

Our discussions span the full spectrum of AI's impact—from semiconductor design and autonomous systems to medical AI, legal ethics, climate sustainability, and the future of work—featuring speakers and collaborators from Amazon, OpenAI, Google, Stanford, Seoul National University, Asan Medical Center, and beyond.

AI Nexus News

MOU Signed with KSWAN Ventures & SAG Korea (24-Aug-2026)

On August 24, 2026, AI Nexus signed an MOU with KSWAN Ventures and SAG Korea at the K-Scaleup Forum in Daegu, establishing a partnership for global startup ecosystem collaboration. The agreement covers nurturing and identifying high-potential entrepreneurs, providing mentoring and pitching opportunities, building a shared mentor pool, co-hosting events and educational programs, and leveraging startup infrastructure and networks.

MOU Signed with the Korean Society for Technology Management & Economics (23-Aug-2026)

On August 23, 2026, AI Nexus signed an MOU with the Korean Society for Technology Management & Economics in Seoul, establishing a partnership for academic exchange and collaborative development in AI and technology management. The agreement covers joint research in AI and technology management, co-hosting academic seminars, forums, and conferences, and fostering mutual institutional development.

AI Nexus at the San Francisco AI Summit (24-Jul-2026)

On July 24, 2026, AI Nexus played a key role in the historic San Francisco AI Summit, where President Lee Jae-myung joined leaders including Jensen Huang (NVIDIA), Sam Altman (OpenAI), Dario Amodei (Anthropic), Hock Tan (Broadcom), Lee Jae-yong (Samsung), Chey Tae-won (SK), Lee Hae-jin (Naver), and Chung Eui-sun (Hyundai Motor) for the “San Francisco AI Declaration” (“샌프란시스코 AI 선언”).

Working closely with the Consulate General of the Republic of Korea in San Francisco for over a month leading up to the event, AI Nexus curated considerable portion of the 150-person invite-only audience. AI Nexus Board Members and leadership team attended the Summit, and Stanford Professor Soh Kim, who moderated the Summit, joined AI Nexus’s July forum as a panelist just days later.

Read more: LinkedIn post on the SF AI Summit

AI Nexus Becomes a Registered 501(c)(3) Nonprofit (15-Jul-2026)

On July 15, 2026, Silicon Valley AI Nexus Inc. officially announced its incorporation as a California nonprofit public benefit corporation and its recognition by the IRS as a tax-exempt organization under Section 501(c)(3). This milestone marks AI Nexus’s transformation from a community forum into an enduring institution—with independent governance, legal standing, and the foundation to pursue its mission for years to come.

Read more: LinkedIn post on 501(c)(3) Nonprofit registration

MOU Signed with Seoul National University College of Engineering (11-Jun-2026)

On June 11, 2026, AI Nexus signed a 3-year MOU with Seoul National University (SNU) College of Engineering, establishing a comprehensive partnership for advanced AI research, education, talent development, and industry-academia collaboration. The agreement covers joint AI research (including responsible AI and privacy-preserving technologies), faculty and talent exchange, next-generation researcher career development, and AI-industry-regulatory policy discussions.
