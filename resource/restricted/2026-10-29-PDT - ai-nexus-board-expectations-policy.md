# Board Expectations Policy

**Adopted by Board resolution on [DATE].**

## 1. Purpose

This policy states what Silicon Valley AI Nexus Inc. expects of each director, and how the Board reviews directors before annual re-election under Section 3.4 of the Bylaws.

## 2. Expectations of Each Director

1. **Forum organizing.** Contribute materially to organizing at least one-third of the Corporation's forums each year (roughly four to five forums at the current cadence of twelve to fifteen). Organizing includes any of: setting the theme, securing speakers, arranging the venue and logistics, arranging food and beverage, preparing announcements, managing registration, supporting on-site operations, or post-event follow-up.
2. **Meetings.** Attend at least 80% of Board meetings, in person or remotely, and respond to time-sensitive Board matters within 48 hours.
3. **Representation.** Represent AI Nexus professionally, promote its forums and initiatives, and facilitate introductions that advance its mission.
4. **Fiduciary duties.** Act in good faith and in the Corporation's best interests, disclose conflicts promptly, and keep confidential matters confidential.

## 3. Annual Review

Before the annual election, the Chair coordinates a review of each director's performance against Section 2 for the preceding year. The review is documented and kept with the Corporation's records. Based on it, the Board may:

- re-elect the director for a new one-year term;
- re-elect conditionally, with specific improvements requested within a remediation period not exceeding 90 days;
- decline to re-elect, in which case service ends at the expiration of the current term; or
- initiate removal under Section 3.6 of the Bylaws where the failures warrant it.

## 4. Amendment

The Board may amend this policy by majority vote at any meeting.
