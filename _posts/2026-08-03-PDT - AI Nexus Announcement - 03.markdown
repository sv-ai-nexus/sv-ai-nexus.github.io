# What We'll Cover

## Defining the problem with data

The labor structure has shifted. Early-career hiring is shrinking fast, while the economics of the one-person builder have never been better. We'll define the problem using the most recent employment data rather than anecdote!

## A structural response &mdash; plus real case studies

Not motivation. Mechanics! What we're building to address the shift, and what has actually worked so far.

## Why now &mdash; and why with [AI Nexus](/)

Over the past year or so, we piloted agentic coding tutoring, and one thing became
unmistakable: <span class="emph">a lot of people can genuinely benefit from
this</span>. People who had never shipped anything were suddenly shipping.

But we also learned where the ceiling is.

Agentic coding solves the *building* problem. It does not solve the
*what-to-build* problem. A working app is not a business. What turns one into the
other is domain insight &mdash; knowing which problem in a specific industry is
painful enough that someone will pay to have it solved.

That insight doesn't come from better prompting. It comes from people who have
spent fifteen years inside healthcare, semiconductors, law, finance, or
manufacturing.

<span style="color: red; font-weight: bold;">Which is exactly who fills the AI Nexus Community.</span>

So this is the collaboration: <strong>Sunbay brings the build infrastructure and a
year of hard-won lessons. [AI Nexus](/) brings the vertical experts.</strong> Put a
builder and a domain expert in the same room, and you get something neither could
produce alone &mdash; a commercialized product with real revenue behind it.

That's the thesis we're testing on August 11th, with 30 people.

# Introducing Sunbay

**Sunbay is an AI builder studio**, led by Korean senior professionals who built their careers in Silicon Valley.

**Builders don't build alone.** Seniors and fellow builders create together and go to market together. It's a community as much as a studio.

Two tracks are planned. The **Builder Track** is our current focus.

**Builder Track**

For people holding down a job and building their own product with AI on weekends. Sunbay provides the US entity, App Store presence, accounting, deployment infrastructure, and anonymous publishing &mdash; with revenue shared between the builder and the studio.

**Trainee Track**

For juniors and students locked out by the experience requirement. Ship real products inside the studio's infrastructure, build a portfolio, and bridge into employment or into the Builder Track.
